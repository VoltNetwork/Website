FAQ

When do I receive my purchase?

Items are delivered to your Minecraft account within a few seconds of payment. Relog if you do not see them straight away.

Can I gift items to another player?

Yes. Tick the gift option at checkout and enter the recipient's Minecraft username.

Rules

Play fair, have fun.

Breaking the rules can lead to a mute, kick or ban depending on severity.

1. Be respectful

No harassment, hate speech or personal attacks, in game or on Discord.

2. No cheating

Hacked clients, x-ray, auto-clickers and exploiting bugs are not allowed. Report bugs to staff instead.

3. No griefing or stealing

Do not destroy or take from other players' claimed builds without permission.

4. No spam or advertising

Keep chat readable and do not promote other servers or services.

5. Keep it safe for everyone

No inappropriate builds, skins or names. Never share personal information.

6. Follow staff instructions

Staff decisions are final in the moment. Appeal on Discord if you think a mistake was made.
